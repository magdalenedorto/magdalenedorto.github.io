#16days4amilliongirls Mural Advocacy Project

As the founder of #16days4amilliongirls, I designed and executed a multi-community mural art project across the North Tongu District of Ghana (Volo, Mepe, Battor, and Aveyime). By utilizing visual community art as a non-verbal advocacy tool, the project engaged 866 young people on anti-SGBV awareness and SRHR rights with support from Oxfam and Global Affairs Canada.
