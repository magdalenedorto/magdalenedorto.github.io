My Chronological Journey

From Mepe to Global Advocacy Stages

Early Foundations

When I Was a Girl in Mepe

My passion for excellence started early in Mepe, North Tongu. From Primary 4 through Junior High, I was a consistent participant in the competitive Fosu Exams. I served proudly as Girls Prefect in both primary and JHS. It was here that the system empowered me, laying the foundation for everything I do today.

Grassroots Leadership

YAM Branch Chair & Champion Spelling Bee

While in JHS, I assumed the role of Branch Chair for the Youth Action Movement (YAM) of PPAG—a position I held for 6 years across 2 terms, leading both self-sponsored and grant-funded community initiatives. During this time, I also emerged as the overall district winner of the Nneka Youth Foundation Inter-District Spelling Bee for North Tongu.

Global Fellowships & Advocacy

National & International Impact

Served on the Governing Council of PPAG (2022–2025) and as YAM National Treasurer. Represented Ghana as an IPPF Global Youth Connect Fellow, Oxfam Power to Choose Youth Forum member, SheDecides Regional Organizer, and Kufuor Scholar.

Present & Beyond

Multilingual Mastery & Tech Integration

Achieved advanced proficiency in Spanish alongside French and native English. Designed this website myself while actively integrating AI and Data Science into youth health mapping, digital citizenship, and international development research.
